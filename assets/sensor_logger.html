🧠 NeuroNexus Sensor Logger

This companion page records phone accelerometer readings and exports a CSV for the research prototype. It does not upload the data anywhere.

Safety and privacy: Only record your own data or data you have explicit permission to collect. Do not use this as a medical test. For gait recording, use a clear level path, secure the phone consistently, and stop if you feel unsteady. Browser sensor access generally requires HTTPS and permission.
