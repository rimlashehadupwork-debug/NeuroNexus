## Brand & Style

This design system expresses a calm, dignified, and scientifically rigorous environment tailored for longevity medicine, gerontology research, and precision biomarker tracking. The experience honors older adults, clinicians, and health-span optimizers alike through extreme legibility, intentional pacing, and zero cognitive fatigue.

### Personality & Emotional Resonance
- **Empathetic & Reassuring:** Interfaces discard frantic alert states and clinical coldness. Visual feedback conveys reassurance, steady progress, and clinical accuracy.
- **Dignified Longevity:** Replaces patronizing accessibility clichés with refined editorial craft, generous touch mechanics, and deep, restorative palettes.
- **Architectural Clarity:** Layouts feel quiet and spacious, balancing data-dense lab panels with restful, open reading surfaces.

### Design Movement
The style synthesizes **Refined Clinical Minimalism** with **Tonal Glass Layers**. It combines high-contrast typography, structural hairline borders (`#e2e8f0`), subtle mineral-tinted backdrops, and soft frosted cards to ensure multi-layered physiological data remains accessible and stress-free.

## Colors

The palette is engineered around restorative deep slates, life-affirming mineral teals, and light-reflecting warm neutrals. It intentionally avoids saturated warning neons in favor of grounded, low-anxiety clinical indicators.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary Deep Slate Navy (`#0d192b`, surface variant `#132238`):** Anchors high-priority headers, structural navigation frames, and decisive actions. Provides maximum contrast against pale foundations.
- **Secondary Alpine & Mineral Teal (`#0d9488`, vibrant `#14b8a6`, muted tint `#ccfbf1`):** Represents optimal health metrics, interactive momentum, and stable biological trends.
- **Neutral Foundation:**
  - Base Canvas: Soft Cream Mist (`#f8fafc`)
  - Sub-surfaces & Wells: Calming Tinted Gray (`#f1f5f9`)
  - Elevated Cards: Pure Crisp White (`#ffffff`)
  - Hairline Separators: Muted Calm Gray (`#e2e8f0`)
  - Secondary Text / Metadata: Slate Muted (`#64748b`)

### Low-Anxiety Status Signaling
- **Optimal / Nominal:** Mineral Teal (`#0d9488`) over Pale Sage (`#f0fdf4`).
- **Observation / Tracking:** Muted Amber Bronze (`#b45309`) over Soft Linen (`#fffbeb`). Replaces stark warning yellows.
- **Action Required / Critical Variance:** Deep Muted Crimson (`#991b1b`) over Gentle Rose-Wash (`#fef2f2`). Never use flashing or neon reds.

## Typography

Typography prioritizes exceptional visual disambiguation across all cognitive conditions and age spectrums.

### Structural Font Pairing
- **Headlines & Interface Chromes (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Provides geometric warmth, contemporary authority, and clear structural hierarchy without appearing sterile or clinical.
- **Body, Medical Metrics & Lab Narratives (Atkinson Hyperlegible Next):** Built specifically for high-acuity legibility. Unambiguous letterforms (differentiating `I`, `l`, `1`, `O`, `0`) prevent dosage misreadings and lab panel misinterpretations.

### Typesetting Directives
- **Enhanced Leading:** Body text incorporates generous line heights (1.55x–1.62x) to support progressive reading scanning for aging visual fields.
- **Metric Formatting:** Numeric values utilize tabular figures where supported, pairing bold Plus Jakarta Sans for the magnitude with Atkinson Hyperlegible Next for clinical units (`mg/dL`, `pg/mL`, `ratio`).

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy uses a calm, rhythmic fluid grid system designed to eliminate claustrophobic dashboard syndrome.

### Grid & Breakpoints
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 fluid columns, `margin-sm` (1rem / 16px), `gutter-sm` (1rem / 16px). Single-column stacked cards ensure tap surfaces remain undisturbed.
- **Tablet (768px – 1024px):** 8 fluid columns, `margin` (2rem / 32px), `gutter` (1.5rem / 24px). Bi-column split views for patient lists and biomarker metrics.
- **Desktop (> 1024px):** 12 fluid columns with a maximum readable container cap of 1440px, `margin-lg` (3rem / 48px), `gutter-lg` (2rem / 32px).

### Spacing Principles
- **Touch Accommodation:** All primary touch targets, interactive cards, and list elements enforce a minimum target height of 48px (ideally 56px for key inputs) to accommodate reduced motor dexterity.
- **Comfortable Section Gaps:** Card grouping uses `space-lg` and `space-xl` to physically separate disparate clinical domains (e.g., Epigenetic Pace vs. Lipid Subfractions), preventing informational bleeding.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses calm tonal surfaces and soft frosted diffusion rather than heavy drop shadows.

### Surface Elevation Hierarchy
- **Level 0 (Canvas Base):** Flat `#f8fafc`. Background on which all workspace activities rest.
- **Level 1 (Clinical Cards & Panels):** Surface `#ffffff` framed with a subtle 1px border of `#e2e8f0`. Shadow: `0px 2px 8px -2px rgba(13, 25, 43, 0.04), 0px 1px 3px 0px rgba(13, 25, 43, 0.02)`.
- **Level 2 (Frosted Modals & Telemetry Overlays):** Surface `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.88)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)` and a 1px border of `rgba(226, 232, 240, 0.8)`. Shadow: `0px 12px 32px -4px rgba(13, 25, 43, 0.06), 0px 4px 12px -2px rgba(13, 25, 43, 0.03)`.
- **Level 3 (Focused Menus & Temporal Popovers):** Surface `#ffffff`. Border 1px `#cbd5e1`. Shadow: `0px 20px 40px -6px rgba(13, 25, 43, 0.08), 0px 8px 16px -4px rgba(13, 25, 43, 0.04)`.

Shadows are tinted with the deep slate primary (`#0d192b`) to maintain cohesion with dark typography and frame lines.

## Shapes

The design system adopts **Level 2 (Rounded)** curvature. 

- **Base Controls & Inputs (`rounded`):** 0.5rem (8px). Delivers structured, precise contours suitable for clinical input and measurement scales.
- **Cards & Data Modules (`rounded-lg`):** 1rem (16px). Creates soft, approachable framing for complex diagnostic datasets.
- **Diagnostic Sheets, Drawers & Highlights (`rounded-xl`):** 1.5rem (24px). Softens the physical boundaries of high-context patient profiles.
- **Status Pills & Metric Badges:** Fully rounded capsule styling (`9999px`) to maintain immediate recognition as discrete categorical markers.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Deep Slate (`#0d192b`) fill, crisp white Atkinson text, 52px height on desktop (48px minimum touch envelope). Hover transitions softly to `#132238` with an inner 1px border tint.
- **Secondary (Teal Action):** Alpine Teal (`#0d9488`) fill, white text. Reserved for regenerative actions, confirming results, or starting assessments.
- **Tertiary / Subdued:** Ghost background, `#0d192b` text, 1px `#e2e8f0` border. On hover, background shifts to `#f1f5f9`.

### Biomarker Cards
- Elevated Level 1 white surface with 16px corner radius.
- Card header features the biomarker title in `headline-sm`, trailing category tag, and last recorded date in `body-sm`.
- Displays values via `metric-value` paired alongside reference interval trackbars colored in muted teal ranges, eliminating sharp alarm indicators.

### Chips & Health Category Filters
- Height 40px, rounded pill format. 
- Inactive state: `#f1f5f9` fill, `#64748b` text, borderless.
- Active state: `#ccfbf1` fill, `#0d9488` text, 1px border of `#14b8a6`.

### Form Fields & Inputs
- Height 52px with 16px Atkinson Hyperlegible body text to prevent visual strain.
- Base border is 1.5px `#e2e8f0` over white fill.
- Focus state brings a sharp 2px mineral teal outline (`#0d9488`) with an ambient 4px soft teal outer glow (`rgba(13, 148, 136, 0.15)`), never default system blues.

### Checkboxes & Segmented Toggles
- Checkbox scale is 22px × 22px (larger than the standard 16px web metric) to support ease of motor control.
- Selected state fills with `#0d9488` containing a bold 2.5px white checkmark.
- Segmented switches feature equal padding, smooth sliding indicators, and clear textual feedback.

### Medical Data Lists
- Structured rows separated by hairline `#e2e8f0` divides.
- Generous internal cell padding (`space-md`). Row hover highlights the entire record in `#f8fafc` without disruptive vertical shifting.
